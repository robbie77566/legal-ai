### Inferences
- A legal-services AI vendor selling to Texas attorneys is within the subtitle's applicability clause (Sec. 551.002) as a "developer"/"deployer" of a product used by Texas residents, but the only duties that bind a private vendor are the intent-based prohibitions (Secs. 552.052, 552.055–.057); the consumer-disclosure duty (Sec. 552.051) applies to governmental agencies and health-care providers, and "consumer" excludes individuals acting in a commercial or employment context — so attorney users are not "consumers" under Sec. 551.001(2).
- Nothing in the enrolled text addresses legal research, drafting, or court filings; the operative constraints on legal AI in Texas come from court rules (KQ6) and professional-conduct rules, not TRAIGA.
