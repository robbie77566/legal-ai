## Key question 4: AI court rules (Fifth Circuit 32.3; N.D. Tex. Judge Starr; E.D. Tex. CV-11; Texas state courts)

### Takeaway
The one primary document obtained is decisive for Texas state practice: on June 26, 2026 the Supreme Court of Texas (Misc. Docket No. 26-9054) gave preliminary approval to amendments to TRCP 13 and TRAP 9.1 and 52.11 that make every signer represent they "reviewed the filing and verified its accuracy, including that the legal citations, quotations, holdings, and record citations are not fabricated," with express proportionate-sanction provisions; comments closed Sept 1, 2026 and the Court "expects the amendments to take effect on October 1, 2026," but as of Oct 18, 2026 no final-approval order appears on the Court's 2026 administrative-orders page. Federal-court pages (ca5, txnd, txed) were all blocked; secondary sources say the Fifth Circuit declined to adopt Rule 32.3 in June 2024, Judge Starr's 2023 certification requirement was never confirmed as rescinded, and E.D. Tex. amended Local Rule CV-11(g) effective Dec 1, 2025.

### Cited Findings (PRIMARY — txcourts.gov, read 2026-10-18)
- Order title/date: "Supreme Court of Texas, Misc. Docket No. 26-9054, Preliminary Approval of Amendments to Texas Rule of Civil Procedure 13 and Texas Rules of Appellate Procedure 9 and 52 and the Repeal of Texas Rule of Civil Procedure 57 … Date: June 26, 2026." Signed by Chief Justice James D. Blacklock and Justices Lehrmann, Devine, Busby, Bland, Huddle, Young, Sullivan, Hawkins. — [Misc. Docket 26-9054 (PDF)](https://txcourts.gov/media/1462936/269054.pdf)
- Procedure: "Comments … should be submitted in writing to rulescomments@txcourts.gov by September 1, 2026. … The Court expects the amendments to take effect on October 1, 2026." The State Bar is directed to email the order to every member and publish it in the Texas Bar Journal. — [Misc. Docket 26-9054 (PDF)](https://txcourts.gov/media/1462936/269054.pdf)
- Proposed TRAP 9.1(d) (new): "By signing a filed document, the signer represents to the court that the signer has: (1) reviewed the filing and verified its accuracy, including that the legal citations, quotations, holdings, and record citations are not fabricated; and (2) to the best of the signer's knowledge, information, and belief formed after reasonable inquiry, the filing is not groundless, brought in bad faith, or brought for the purpose of harassment." — [Misc. Docket 26-9054 (PDF)](https://txcourts.gov/media/1462936/269054.pdf)
- Proposed TRAP 9.1(f) Sanctions: court may sanction "on motion or its own initiative … after providing the signer with notice and a reasonable opportunity to respond"; "Available sanctions may include reprimand, contempt, striking of the filing in whole or in part, dismissal of proceedings, costs, or attorney's fees. - Comment to 2026 change (TRAP 9): "Paragraph (d)(1) is adopted principally to address concerns about the improper use of artificial intelligence in filings, which increases the risk of fabricated authorities, quotations, and holdings appearing in court filings.
