# The counsel packet

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Plan:** `docs/implementation/persona_rollout_plan.md` §3 (writ practitioner, PD appellate attorney), §5 E, January slice · **Trust posture:** `docs/business_case/attorney_offering_and_pricing.md` ("the verification posture should ship in the packet").

A counsel matter's deliverable is a packet built for a brief, not a family letter. Part B comes first; the Part A client letter is optional and last, so the lawyer decides whether to hand it over. The packet emits **record citations and doctrine categories, never case citations** — that is what keeps it on the right side of every sanction the research found, and the first-page disclosure says so in as many words.

Renderer: `packages/reports/counsel-packet.ts` (`renderCounselPacketPdf`, `counselPacketSections` — the same content as JSON, in packet order, for the web page and the tests). Disclosure instruments: `packages/reports/disclosures.ts`. Routes: `apps/api/src/routes/counsel-packet.ts`; input built by `apps/api/src/services/counsel-packet.service.ts`. Template version `counsel-v1`, stamped on the cover beside the released report's own template (`AB-v2`). The family renderer moved to `packages/reports/family-report.ts` unchanged; the palette, footer and header both renderers share live in `pdf-helpers.ts`.

### The verified-against-record mark

The renderer never computes the mark. The api runs FR-7 (`verifyFindings`, the same call the family report makes at every render) and hands the renderer two lists, `findings` (verified) and `droppedFindings`; the mark is true for every finding in the first list because it is in that list, and the second list is printed under "Dropped at verification" and in the 9.1(d) log as `dropped`. A finding cannot be marked verified by a flag, and a dropped one cannot vanish.

## Routes

Both at `/counsel/matters/:caseId/…`, under the matter routes' hooks: `counsel_surfaces` flag (404 when off), `PROFESSIONAL` with a live seat (403), organisation-scoped (another organisation's matter is 404). `409 { reason: 'NO_RELEASED_REPORT' }` before QA has released a report — the packet is built from the released report version the family route serves (`?version=` picks an earlier one).

- `GET …/packet.json` → `{ caseId, reportId, versionNo, templateVersion: 'counsel-v1', sections }`
- `GET …/packet.pdf` → the PDF, `attachment; filename="counsel-packet-<caseId>-v<n>.pdf"`

Switches: `clientLetter=1`, `wdtex=1`. Every fetch writes a `PACKET_DOWNLOAD` audit row with `{ format, versionNo, clientLetter, wdtex, organisationId }` — ids and switches only, never initials, a cause number or a county.

The ops case file (`GET /ops/cases/:id/file`) now says which deliverable a case gets: `case.variant: 'family' | 'counsel'` (derived from the Matter row; no new column) and `case.matterVehicle`; the ops case page shows a COUNSEL PACKET chip. Workstream F's matter page links to `packet.pdf`.

## Sealed attorney packet (2026-10-23, UPL review E2; counsel question 3)

The **family** product's attorney content — not the counsel-tier packet above. Finding E2 of `docs/compliance/upl_review_2026-10-23.md`: Part B, the preservation call, the harm standard, the vehicle and the develop steps were delivered to the family inline. Whether a family may receive Part B at all is counsel question 3; until counsel answers, the conservative default ships: the family report carries Part A, and the attorney content is one separate document the family downloads sealed and hands to licensed counsel, or shares by link.

- **Flag** `family_part_b` (default off; `20261023000038_family_part_b_flag`; `staff_console.md` §10). Read once per render in `loadVerifiedReport` (`apps/api/src/routes/intake.ts`), marked `// UPL review 2026-10-23: counsel decision pending (question 3)`. **Off:** `GET /cases/:id/report` omits `partBText`, `preserved`, `harmStandard`, `vehicle` and `develop` from every finding (`dependsOn` stays: the family can act on a missing record); the family PDF omits the per-finding "For an attorney" block, the labels line, "To develop", "For counsel — issues by priority" and "Investigation checklist" and prints one section **"For your lawyer"** (both languages, `FOR_YOUR_LAWYER_COPY`); the report page replaces the Part B folds and the priority section with the same card and two actions, *Download the attorney packet (sealed)* and *Share with a lawyer* (`apps/web/lib/content/attorney-packet.ts`, en/es, in the parity test). **On:** exactly the pre-split behaviour — `apps/api/tests/part-b-split.integration.test.ts` holds an inline snapshot of the payload shape taken before the change.
- **Renderer** `packages/reports/attorney-packet.ts`: `attorneyPacketSections(input)` → `cover` (the cover line *"For the attorney. Prepared for the family to hand to licensed counsel; information about a court record, not legal advice."* in English and Spanish, the statutory statement `NOT_A_LAW_FIRM` from `packages/case-lifecycle/brand.ts`, the R-7 notice), `case_summary`, `posture` (the bottom line's lawyer line), `time_computation` (the §2244(d) rule, the dates given and the arithmetic as data lines — no "CLOSED", no verdict), `part_b` (Part B text, preservation · vehicle · harm, develop, depends-on, every citation), `priority`, `checklist`. `renderAttorneyPacketPdf` draws exactly those sections; template `attorney-v1`. Never touches the database.
- **Routes.** `GET /cases/:id/attorney-packet.pdf` (case member; same FR-7 run and verified snapshot as the report; `?version=`), always available whatever the flag says. `GET /shared/:token` keeps its flat fields and adds `sections` from the same `buildAttorneyPacketInput` (`apps/api/src/services/attorney-packet.service.ts`); the share page `apps/web/app/(daybreak)/shared/[token]/page.tsx` renders the sections and nothing else, so the link and the download cannot drift. The shared notice no longer claims a reviewer's approval.
- **Advice-language lint** (E3, recommendation 11): `apps/api/src/services/advice-lint.ts` — `lintAdvice(text, { audience: 'family' | 'counsel' })`. Family text: second-person imperatives and *should, must file, file a, you qualify, you are eligible, viable, winnable, meritorious, you have N days, your deadline, will win, will lose, cannot win, grounds for relief, you should contact* ("a lawyer can tell you" allowed). Counsel text: only *the defendant should / you should* and outcome predictions (*will prevail, will be granted*); Strickland and preservation vocabulary passes. The pipeline stores the hits on `Finding.lintFlags` (`{ adviceLintHit, partA, partB }`, null when clean); auto-QA (`auto-qa.service.ts`) re-lints every Part A live and holds the case (`advice wording in N finding(s)`) so a reviewer's rewrite clears it; the QA finding card shows an **advice wording** badge with the phrases. Prompt: the fixed system prompt gained *"You describe what the record contains. You never advise, never tell anyone what to file or when, never predict an outcome, and never address the family or the defendant directly."* and the v2 contract's Part A asks for a description of the cited page with no advice, instruction, prediction or "you should". The Strickland, preservation and severity instructions for Part B are unchanged (counsel reads it).
- **Tests.** `packages/reports/tests/attorney-packet.test.ts` (sealed family PDF, packet sections ↔ PDF text, statutory statement), `apps/api/tests/part-b-split.integration.test.ts` (flag off/on, the packet route, the share link's sections, the lint hold under `AUTO_APPROVE=1`), `apps/api/tests/advice-lint.unit.test.ts`, web `ReportSealedPartB.test.tsx`, `SharedPacket.test.tsx`, `QaAdviceBadge.test.tsx`.
